---
type: principle
title: One tool, one job
---

Each tool does one thing well and is named for that thing. No settings
panel that turns a date calculator into a timezone converter into a
calendar app.

This keeps every tool a single, disposable file: easy to read start to
finish, easy to rewrite from scratch instead of patched into something it
wasn't designed to be. When a second, related need shows up, that's a new
tool, not a feature flag on the first one.
